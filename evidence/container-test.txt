Production improvements :

docker run -d `
  --name logikality-api `
  --read-only `
  --cap-drop ALL `
  --security-opt no-new-privileges:true `
  --pids-limit 100 `
  --memory 256m `
  --cpus 0.5 `
  -p 127.0.0.1:8080:8080 `
  logikality-document-api:v1


These options introduce additional restrictions:
- --read-only: Prevents writes to the container's root filesystem.
- --cap-drop ALL: Removes Linux capabilities.
- --security-opt no-new-privileges:true: Prevents privilege escalation through mechanisms such as setuid.
- --pids-limit 100: Limits the number of processes.
- --memory 256m and --cpus 0.5: Constrain resource consumption.
